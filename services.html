Services

Pick your flavor of whimsy.

Whimsy Entertainment offers professional party entertainment serving Northeast Ohio (near Cleveland & Columbus), with availability for Detroit and Pittsburgh bookings, and nationwide for select events: kids birthday party packages, balloon twisting, face painting, juggling workshops, circus sideshow, LED and fire performances. Mix and match services to build the perfect entertainment lineup for your party, festival, school program, bar or club night, corporate event or grand opening.

Kids’ Parties & Family Events
  • Children’s birthday parties with clown characters, juggling, silly games and interactive fun.
  • Giant bubble shows that fill the yard or park with massive shimmering bubbles.
  • Face painting and glitter accents with kid-friendly designs and character looks.
  • Balloon twisting for swords, animals, flowers, hats and custom wearable balloon art.
  • Perfect for backyard parties, family reunions, school fun days and community celebrations.
Workshops & Community Programs
  • Beginner juggling workshops available for kids, teens and adults — including Saria “Blu” Braska’s “Catch the Rhythm” juggling workshop.
  • Hands-on skills sessions focused on juggling basics, coordination and confidence-building.
  • Great option for schools, libraries and camps that want an interactive juggling experience with room for participation.
  • Can be paired with roaming clown or mime characters for festivals, fairs and public events.
  • Custom workshop plans available for after-school clubs, youth groups and arts organizations on request.
Stage & Fire Shows
  • Fire eating and fire breathing (weather, safety and venue permitting).

Custom & Corporate Events

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What variety of events do you perform at?

Available for children’s birthdays, holiday parties, family events, school shows, library events, conventions, festivals, corporate events, nightlife and concerts.

What acts do you offer?

Roaming juggling patterns, balloon twisting, balloon creations, face painting, flow arts, clowning/mime and giant bubbles.

For a mature audience I offer sideshow; fire eating, fire breathing, fire spinning, human blockhead, mental floss, and bed of nails.

Do you travel outside of Ohio?

Yes, I’m based in Northeast Ohio, but can travel anytime across the surrounding states such as Michigan, and Pennsylvania. Willing to travel anywhere within reason!

Are fire and sideshow acts safe?

Safety is always the priority. Fire eating, fire breathing and fire object manipulation are only performed where venue rules, weather, space and safety conditions allow. Circus sideshow acts such as blockhead and bed of nails are performed by an experienced professional with over 10 years on stage. We are happy to discuss what is appropriate for your audience.

What do you need from the venue or host?

Requirements depend on the acts you choose. For most kids party and roaming sets (bubbles, balloons, face painting, juggling and clowning) we mainly need enough space to safely move around, a small setup area and access to shade or indoor space in extreme weather. Fire and LED shows may require a clear performance area, distance from the audience and permission from the venue.

How far in advance should we book?

Weekends and holidays can book up quickly, especially for children’s parties and festivals. Booking a few weeks to a few months in advance is ideal, but last-minute openings do happen. Contact us with your date, time and location and we’ll let you know current availability.
